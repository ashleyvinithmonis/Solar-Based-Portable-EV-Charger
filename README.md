# Solar-Based-Portable-EV-Charger
Electric vehicles are becoming more common because they help reduce pollution and the use of petrol and diesel. However, finding a convenient way to charge EVs is still a challenge for many users. Most charging stations depend on electricity from the power grid, and charging facilities are not easily available in every location. Solar energy is a renewable source of energy that can be used to reduce dependence on grid electricity. Since sunlight is freely available during the daytime, we wanted to explore how solar energy could be used in a small portable charging system. This idea motivated us to work on the project “Solar-Based Portable EV Charging System.” Along with developing the charging arrangement, we wanted to monitor important electrical values such as voltage, current, power, and energy. We also wanted to show these readings in a mobile app so that users could easily check the charging details on their phones.
